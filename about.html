Since 2021

It started with a brick oven

We built our first oven by hand in a small shop in Gujrat. The rule then is the rule now: if it isn't fresh, it doesn't go on the pizza. No frozen dough, no shortcuts, no gas.

The craft

Slow dough, fast fire

Our dough rests for two days so it turns light and airy. Each base is stretched by hand, topped with San Marzano tomato and mozzarella made that morning, then fired for ninety seconds until the crust blisters.
